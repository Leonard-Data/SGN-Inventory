The national two-tier province/commune code list is embedded as an idempotent seed migration (`20261014075435_vietnamese_admin_units_reference.sql`) drawn from the pinned upstream releases above: the 2025-07-01 baseline window carries the pre-2026 official names (later upstream diacritic corrections are kept), and the dated 2026-04-30, 2026-09-01 and 2026-09-20 windows carry the v5.2.0 names, each with one half-open validity window per official name. A legacy district level (696 districts, with its 63 provinces and 10,051 wards) is seeded only for the half-open `[2025-01-04, 2025-07-01)` pre-2025 window by `20261021060602_legacy_admin_units_reference.sql`, and the official old→new crosswalk by `20261021080934_admin_unit_crosswalks_official_ingest.sql` (10,622 rows: 63 province-level, 5 district-level, 10,554 ward-level, covering 10,023 of the 10,051 legacy wards and leaving 28 without a row, from the pinned conversion table `dev/seed-data/2025-07/BangChuyendoiĐVHCmoi_cu_khong_merge.csv`, sha256 `21da94f59a498d03deda1f0882e71f65cd3b7c855bbd6c760ba72f6b6a94ee46`). Both of those come from sunshine-tech/VietnamProvinces rather than the two-tier vendor above, whose release carries no district table and therefore no three-tier snapshot; both seed factual rows only, and their declared GPL-3.0-or-later licence is an open legal item. Nothing seedable maps an individual property, and nothing maps it automatically: a crosswalk row is candidate evidence and never verification, and the crosswalk is migration-only after `20261021080935_admin_unit_crosswalks_write_boundary.sql`. Raw workbook content, employee password values, owner contacts and attachment bytes are not included in the repository package.
